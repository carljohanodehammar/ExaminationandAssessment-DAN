förutsebarhet

“i rättsliga angelägenheter”. Predictability in legal matters. It requires clear rules that are

1

Clear

The student can tell what is allowed, what is not, and what is assessed.

2

Published

In writing, at least two weeks before the examination (BRJU), in direct connection with the task.

3

Applied loyally and correctly

Part 2 · Measurable rules

A rule you cannot measure is a recommendation

RuleWhat is allowed, what is not, what is assessed
ConditionsStated in writing, two weeks before
FormatA planned moment that produces evidence
CriteriaAssess what shows the rule was followed
AssessmentDocumented, so it can be reviewed

The last four are the phrase bank's four notes.

The test: how would you show afterwards, to DAN or on review, that the rule was followed or broken?

Cannot be measuredCan be measuredHow you measure it

The text must be your own.

You may not submit text, code, image or analysis that you cannot explain.A2 B

State in the instruction that the task may be followed up with questions about the content, and that this is part of the examination. Five minutes per student is enough. Note what the student could account for.

You may not rely entirely on AI to write your code.

The code must be written by you. You may not use AI to generate functions, classes or structures that you then submit.B3 B

A short code walk-through in a controlled setting. Criteria assess the choice of solution and its justification, not only that the code runs.

Use AI responsibly in the analysis.

You may not use AI to carry out the analysis. Calculations and analysis steps you perform yourself and report.B4 B

Require reported intermediate steps. A correct final value without them cannot earn full marks. The rule becomes assessable without proving anything about tools.

Jönköping University
The core principle of the talk. Rättssäkerhet requires that compliance can be shown afterwards against stated criteria. The phrase bank's four notes, conditions, format, criteria and assessment, are how you get there. In every measurable version, no one has to prove anything about AI: the evidence is something the student produced or showed.

Part 2 · Beyond the declaration

Talk is cheap: change the task, not just the text

Discursive change

Rules students can choose to ignore

Traffic-light labels, AI declarations, longer instructions. They communicate, but cannot enforce. The authors call it an enforcement illusion.

→
Structural change

The task itself produces the evidence

Change what students do and what is assessed, so validity is built into the mechanics of the examination.

Corbin, Dawson & Liu 2025
Better than a declaration: make the AI use part of what is assessed
1

Annotated exchange

The student submits a key exchange with the tool and comments in the margin: what was kept, what was rejected, and why. The judgement is assessed.

2

Version trail

Drafts in version history, or commits for code. The trail is read alongside the product, and shows how the work grew.

3

Critique the machine

Students get an AI answer and must find what is wrong, missing or generic, and improve it against the course literature.

4

Defend the choices

A short live conversation where the questions are drawn from the student's own log or trail. It cannot be prepared generically.

Jönköping University
Corbin, Dawson and Liu (2025) distinguish discursive changes, which depend on instructions students can ignore, from structural changes to the task itself. A declaration is discursive: in Gonsalves' study 74 percent did not declare. The four alternatives make AI use visible as work that is assessed. They correspond to phrase bank I2 (the work log as assessed material), process documentation and the closing conversation.
Part 2 · Innovative examination, with research behind it

New formats, and what the research says

Tested

Interactive oral in a work scenario

A simulated professional conversation, such as a client meeting. More authentic assessment was linked to less anxiety and less misconduct, and the model scaled to hundreds of students.

Sotiriadou et al. 2020
Tested

Interactive oral at scale

722 students, 40 percent of the course grade. No significant differences by gender, international status or language, or between assessors.

Davey et al. 2025
Established

Programmatic assessment

Many low-stakes data points across a programme. High-stakes decisions rest on the aggregate, never on a single submission.

van der Vleuten & Schuwirth 2005
Established

Evaluative judgement

Students judge the quality of work: their own, their peers' and the machine's. The judgement is what is assessed.

Tai et al. 2018 · Bearman & Luckin 2020
Emerging

Assessment twins

An open task and a secured task test the same outcome, close in time. Each is read against the other.

Roe, Perkins & Giray 2026
Emerging

Error-correction tasks

Students find, explain and fix planted errors. The AI found only 50 to 79 percent of them. Small samples so far.

Renjewski & Strasser 2026

In a review of ten studies, none could show both validated measures of AI use and a link to the learning outcomes. Choose by the outcome, not by the trend.

Jönköping University
Tested means evaluated in practice with students; established means a well-developed framework with a long research base; emerging means early studies. Programmatic assessment comes from medical education and fits programme-level work under the rector's decision. Evaluative judgement is the competence that AI makes central: knowing what good looks like. Ask: which of these could you pilot next term?

Part 3 · Regulations in force from 1 January 2025
It is always DAN, never the teacher, that decides whether a student tried to deceive.
Point 11 (i)
Deceive

Attempting to deceive with prohibited aids or in another way when a performance is assessed. Prohibited AI use falls here.

Point 30
Report

A grounded suspicion shall be reported without delay. You do not need to be certain.

Point 12
≤ 0 months

Warning, or suspension of up to six months in total. Never your own sanctions.

Intent
Proven

DAN must establish intent to deceive. Misunderstanding or carelessness leads to acquittal.

An acquittal is not a failure. It shows the system works. Wait with the grade until DAN has decided.

Jönköping University
No measure later than two years after the offence. Compulsory withdrawal is a separate matter and never follows from a cheating report. The grade is the examiner's decision, the measure is DAN's.

Part 3 · Holds for every rule in the bank

Five preconditions that belong in the study guide

1

Notify in time

In writing, at least two weeks before (BRJU). A rule not notified in time cannot be tested.

2

No rule is not permission

Rules are set per component. If one is missing, the student asks the examiner.

3

Support decisions go first

Never write a rule that makes an exception for special pedagogical support impossible.

4

No tools from JU

JU provides no AI tools for students. A task that needs one must be solvable for free.

5

The student answers

For everything submitted, including what the tool contributed.

Syllabus wordingJ1
